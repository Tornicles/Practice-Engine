ENGINE 9 — COURSE & PRODUCT SYSTEM
9.1 Purpose

Engine 9 converts validated methodology and expertise into repeatable, teachable, scalable products.

It answers:

How do we package what the coach knows into something another person can learn, use, complete, and achieve a measurable result from?

This is different from Engine 5.

Engine 5 creates the intellectual property.

Engine 9 productizes the intellectual property.

Engine 10 is the measurement and state-evaluation engine for the entire Practice Engine.

It answers:

What is actually happening?

This is one of the most important underlying engines because the other engines generate enormous amounts of activity and evidence.

Without Engine 10, the system becomes a collection of records.

12.15 AI safety architecture

The intelligence system needs several boundaries.

Boundary 1 — Source

Know what information the AI is using.

Boundary 2 — Evidence

Distinguish evidence from inference.

Boundary 3 — Authority

Do not fabricate credentials, outcomes, clients, or expertise.

Boundary 4 — System truth

AI recommendations should not automatically overwrite system-computed states.

Boundary 5 — User control

Actions that materially change records should have controlled workflows.

The most important architectural principle

The final Practice Engine should therefore not be twelve dashboards.

It should be:

12 engines + shared data model + evidence layer + workflow layer + statistics/condition layer + AI orchestration layer.

The dashboards are merely the interfaces through which the coach interacts with those engines.

That distinction is what turns the current Practice Engine foundation into a genuine Coach Development Operating System rather than a collection of AI-assisted productivity features.
