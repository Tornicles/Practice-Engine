### The key structure

The menu should visually communicate the progression:

**BECOME A BETTER COACH**
→ Coach Lab
→ Practice Simulator
→ Delivery Studio

**TURN EXPERIENCE INTO EXPERTISE**
→ Experience Vault
→ Methodology

**BECOME A RECOGNIZED COACH**
→ Brand Lab
→ Content Studio

**TURN EXPERTISE INTO IP**
→ Course Builder
→ Offers & Products
→ Proof & Outcomes

**YOUR PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY**
→ Coach Passport

I would **not** put the 1,000 Prompts in the menu. It should remain the hidden intelligence/orchestration layer powering these areas.
