Practice Engine — Detailed Architecture
Engines 5–8

These four engines form the commercialization and authority layer of Practice Engine.

They take the output of Engines 1–4:

practice → client delivery → experience → expertise

and turn it into:

methodology → authority → audience → revenue.

The dependency structure is:

Experience → Expertise
        │
        ↓

        ├───────────────┐
        ↓               ↓
ENGINE 6           ENGINE 7
Brand &            Content &
Authority          Audience
        │               │
        └───────┬───────┘
                ↓
             ENGINE 8
        Offers & Commercial
ENGINE 5 — METHODOLOGY & IP SYSTEM
5.1 Purpose

Engine 5 converts validated expertise into a coherent, proprietary methodology that can be used repeatedly across coaching, content, courses, products, and commercial offers.

The fundamental transformation is:

Experience
   ↓
Lesson
   ↓
Pattern
   ↓
Principle
   ↓
Method
   ↓
Framework
   ↓
Methodology
   ↓
IP Asset

This engine answers:

What does this coach uniquely know, how do they work, why does it work, and what reusable intellectual property has been developed from evidence?

5.9 Contradiction and limitation system

The methodology engine should actively look for exceptions.

Example:

Principle:
"Direct challenge improves accountability."

Evidence:
Works in high-accountability environments.

Contradictory evidence:
Creates resistance in low-trust environments.

Result:
Principle requires contextual condition.

The system should therefore support:

Supporting evidence
Contradicting evidence
Conditions
Exceptions
Limitations
Boundary conditions

This makes methodology substantially more credible.

THE CROSS-ENGINE DATA FLOW

The most important relationships are:

From	Produces	Feeds
Engine 4	Expertise	Engine 5
Engine 5	Methodology/IP	Engines 6, 7, 8
Engine 6	Positioning/Proof	Engines 7, 8
Engine 7	Audience intelligence	Engines 4, 6, 8
Engine 8	Client/market intelligence	Engines 3, 4, 5

So the system becomes:

EXPERIENCE
    ↓
EXPERTISE
    ↓
METHODOLOGY
    ↓
AUTHORITY
    ↓
CONTENT
    ↓
AUDIENCE
    ↓
OFFER
    ↓
CLIENT
    ↓
OUTCOME
    ↓
EXPERIENCE

That is the actual Practice Engine flywheel.

The critical architectural point is that Engines 5–8 should not become content-management, CRM, branding, or sales tools bolted onto the side of Practice Engine. Their purpose is to commercialize the coach's validated expertise, while every commercial result feeds new evidence back into the development and expertise engines.
